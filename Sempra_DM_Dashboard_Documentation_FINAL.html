🎯 1. WHAT We Are Doing & WHY

What We Are Doing: Sempra is executing an enterprise-wide SAP S/4HANA migration across three sequential waves: DM1 (Mock 1), DM2 (Mock 2), and DM3 (Dry Run / Cutover). Hundreds of millions of critical customer, financial, and asset records are extracted, transformed, and loaded across 7,700+ database tables.

The Manual Challenge Solved: Historically, verifying data completeness consumed so many hours and days of exhausting manual spreadsheet work. Teams had to manually cross-reference 6+ massive Excel sheets with brittle VLOOKUPs. This platform replaces all manual labor with an automated, sub-10-second reconciliation engine guaranteeing 100% Zero Data Loss.
